# Sleek Odhikar landing page plan

## Outcome
Replace the content-heavy homepage with a polished, full-viewport welcome experience that immediately establishes Odhikar’s identity and opens a focused choice panel containing exactly the three existing journeys.

## Homepage experience
- Create a clean, high-impact visual composition using Odhikar’s existing navy, green, amber, and urgent semantic colors, with refined typography, restrained motion, and a subtle civic/legal visual motif.
- Keep the Odhikar name, a short Bangla-first welcome line, the বাংলা/EN control, discreet staff access, and essential emergency access visible without recreating the full public-site navigation or long sections.
- Present the three options in an accessible modal-style choice panel: victim support, witnessed-crime/GD drafting, and legal knowledge.
- Make the panel open on arrival, dismissible without trapping users, and easy to reopen from the main call-to-action.
- Preserve each option’s existing route and functionality unchanged.

## Global-standard safeguards
- Use semantic links and dialog behavior with clear keyboard focus, Escape/close support, meaningful labels, and large mobile touch targets.
- Keep brief trust context near the choices: private handling, human review for victim cases, no automatic filing, and emergency numbers.
- Ensure the composition works on small phones, tablets, and wide desktop screens, including reduced-motion preferences.
- Retain unique homepage metadata and the existing public shell behavior needed by all other public pages.

## Verification
- Confirm all three choices navigate to the correct existing flows.
- Confirm Bangla/English switching updates every homepage label and remains remembered.
- Check modal keyboard behavior, mobile layout, desktop layout, and browser console errors.
